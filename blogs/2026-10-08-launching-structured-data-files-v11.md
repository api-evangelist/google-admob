Today we’re announcing the general availability of Structured Data Files v11 . All users can now use v11 when downloading and uploading SDFs in the Display & Video 360 interface. SDF v11 includes the following changes: Expanded the availability of Lookalike audience inclusion and exclusion targeting to all ad groups except for those under YouTube Ad Sequence line items.
