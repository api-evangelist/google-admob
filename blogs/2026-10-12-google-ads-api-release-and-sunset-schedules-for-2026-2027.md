Last year, we shifted to a monthly release schedule for the Google Ads API to provide you faster API access to Ads features available in the Google Ads UI . As we enter a new agentic era where speed and agility are paramount, we are thrilled to accelerate our launch timelines even further - moving to two Google Ads API releases per month starting from version v26! This faster cadence gives your automated agents and workflows near-instant access to cutting-edge features, allowing you to stay ahead of the curve, reduce time-to-market, and innovate with unprecedented efficiency.
