Today we’re announcing the general availability of Structured Data Files v10.1 . All users can now use v10.1 when downloading and uploading SDFs in the Display & Video 360 interface. SDF v10.1 includes the following changes: The ability to specify whether a YouTube video asset identified in a Video ID or Related Videos column contains content that was created or edited with AI.
