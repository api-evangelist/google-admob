What is changing? On August 31, 2026, we are aligning Shopping campaigns with Performance Max for Retail campaigns by enabling Local Inventory Ads (LIA) by default. Previously, developers and advertisers had to explicitly set the enable_local field to true within a campaign's ShoppingSetting in order to serve products in their LIA feed.
