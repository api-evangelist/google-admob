Today we’re announcing three unversioned changes to the Display & Video 360 API and Structured Data Files that will take effect in October 2026 and may impact your existing integrations. These changes will take place on the following dates and have been fully detailed on our Announced Deprecations page: Starting October 1, 2026 : You will no longer be able to exclude specific digital content labels using targeting , impacting available TARGETING_TYPE_DIGITAL_CONTENT_LABEL_EXCLUSION targeting options in the Display & Video 360 API and valid values for the Digital Content Labels - Exclude column
